A change of scenery

Find your quiet place.

Same little escape. A different kind of meadow.

A moment for yourself

Less doing. More being.

1

Step into the meadow

Move your mouse, or drag a finger through the grass. It bends and gently springs back. Butterflies flutter away when your hand gets close.

2

Make it your moment

Switch between a bright morning and a firefly-filled night. Touch the tree in the morning to send birds into the sky. At night, two owls follow your hand with their eyes. Tap the rain cloud for wet grass, raindrops and splashes.

3

Take a little break

The words fade away. Follow the breathing circle, watch the grass, and let the timer take care of the rest.

Everything runs in this file. No account, tracking, or internet connection needed. Keyboard: N switches the light; S toggles sound; R toggles rain; B touches the tree; Space starts or ends a break; arrow keys brush the meadow.
